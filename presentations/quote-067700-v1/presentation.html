Why now

A failing switcher with no room left.

The existing ATEM 2 M/E Production Studio 4K uses 19 of its 20 SDI inputs and all 6 aux outputs. Adding the second NewBlue key/fill channel alone needs more inputs than it has.

Today’s risk

The CMR switcher is experiencing operational failures. Every source on the dais, every Zoom participant, every caption that reaches residents passes through it.

Replacing it like-for-like would leave the City with a full switcher on day one. The Constellation doubles the inputs and quadruples the aux outputs in the same 2RU of rack space.

Cameras to broadcast

The core of every meeting. The five Panasonic PTZ cameras stay, and so does the desk PTZ controller. The switcher underneath them is new.

  1. CAM 1–5 feed the Constellation over native SDI. No conversion needed.
  2. The operator cuts on the Advanced Panel 10. Program audio comes in on balanced ¼″ TRS (XLR adapters included).
  3. Aux 4 sends program to the EEG caption engine and on to the broadcast channels.
  4. Aux 3, 5 and 6 feed the Ikegami room monitor, Router 2 clean feed and the CMR room feed. All cameras show on MV1.

Hybrid Zoom meeting

Remote participants see the meeting, and the room sees them. If the Zoom Room fails, the Control PC is already wired as the backup.

  1. The Logitech Zoom Room return comes in through a Decimator to switcher input 6.
  2. Aux 1 sends the meeting picture back to the Zoom Room through a Decimator.
  3. Aux 2 feeds the Control PC, which runs the Q-SYS UCI and is the backup Zoom PC (HDMI in).
  4. Zoom is shown on MV1 next to the cameras.

On-air graphics

NewBlue Fusion 3 Extreme replaces the current character generator for lower thirds, speaker IDs, agenda items and full-screen graphics.

  1. Two SDI fill/key channels use four switcher inputs.
  2. They drive DSK 1 and DSK 2, so two graphics layers can be on air at once.
  3. Graphics ride program out through Aux 4 → EEG captions → broadcast.
  4. Fill and key are monitored on MV2.

Dais (Televic)

The four Televic sources — Mayor, Manager, Council and Clerk/Vice — keep their switcher inputs (16–19).

  1. Each Televic output gets its own Decimator 12G-CROSS into the switcher.
  2. Sources are labeled and shown on MV2 with the ME2 program and preview.
  3. Whatever the operator takes to program goes out through Aux 4 → EEG → broadcast.

Presentations

Staff presentations, PCs and Crestron NVX content keep working as they do today.

  1. PRES-PC, PC2, Plate HDMI and NVX 1–2 each go through a Decimator. Plate SDI 1–2 go straight in.
  2. Every Constellation input does format conversion, so mismatched sources are converted in the switcher.
  3. PCs show on MV1, NVX on MV2, and program goes to broadcast through Aux 4.

The schematic is simplified. Final converter placement and assignments are confirmed against the existing connector types on site.

Cutover plan

One planned maintenance window. No surprises.

We document the system before we touch it and schedule the cutover with City staff. We test every path before handing it back.

1
Document
Review switcher settings, source assignments, aux routing and CG connections. Preserve accessible configurations.
2

3
Remove
Label every connection, then remove the old switcher, panel and CG. Equipment is left at a City-designated spot on site.
4
Install
Rack the Constellation and NewBlue, set the panel at the desk, and place all 18 Decimators.
5
Configure
Set format, inputs, multiviews, aux routing, DSKs, audio levels and converter settings.
6
Commission
Test every path, including captions and backup Zoom, then walk through it with City staff.
