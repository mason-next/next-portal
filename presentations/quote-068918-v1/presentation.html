01 · Solution Summary

A production-grade auditorium that one person can run

Mason Technologies will furnish, install, integrate, program, tune, commission and train on a complete AV modernization of the Building 1 Auditorium, for theater, concerts, jazz, chorus, lectures, VIP events, video production, live streaming and Zoom. The existing high-output projector and the stage XLR lines that pass testing are reused.

A PA built for music

Left and right arrays of four YME iA211X, a center CL 114-30 for speech, and four dual 18 in subwoofers at the stage, with front fills, rear delays and booth monitors so every seat is covered.

Performers hear themselves

Permanent side fills on both sides of the stage, two passive wedges at the downstage edge and two self-powered wedges midstage, all fed from the console.

A real production console

A Yamaha DM7 Compact with the DM7 Control fader extension and a Rio3224-D3 Dante stage box: 72 channels for theater, concerts, jazz and chorus.

Twelve wireless channels

MDC's Shure SLX-D+ system, with all 12 channels reaching both the console and Q-SYS over Dante. Twelve DPA CORE+ headsets, four lavaliers, and three suspended choir microphones.

Six Sony 4K PTZ cameras

Five fixed SRG-A40 cameras with AI tracking, plus a portable sixth camera on a tripod with connection points at stage left and stage right, and a Sony RM-IP500 joystick controller.

Two workflows, all in 4K

A Blackmagic ATEM 2 M/E Constellation 4K and a Videohub 40×40 12G router: M/E 1 for cameras, recording, streaming and Zoom; M/E 2 for presentations, each with its own panel.

Bigger images

A new 220 in Draper tab-tensioned screen for the existing 15,000-lumen projector, and two 110 in BlueSquareX 4K displays in place of the side projection.

Record, stream and Zoom at once

A HyperDeck Studio 4K Pro, a Blackmagic Streaming Encoder 4K and a Zoom Room, each on its own router output, with the house mix embedded in the program feed.

One person can run it

Q-SYS presets on a 10 in touch panel and Stream Deck macros through Bitfocus Companion. Routine events run without the console; room tuning stays locked.

02 · System Design

How every signal travels

Audio rides Dante and AES3 between the console, Q-SYS and the amplifiers. Video stays on the 12G-SDI workflow MDC's staff already know, at 4K30 from camera to screen, with HDMI only at the edges.

One frame rate

Cameras run at 29.97 frames per second, and every Decimator and the ATEM are set to match, so nothing stutters between sources.

24 Decimators

One at every HDMI source and display on the router, converting and scaling to 4K30, with two spares on site so a failed unit is swapped in minutes.

Room to grow

The new 40×40 12G router uses about 20 inputs and 25 outputs, and the ATEM has six spare 12G-SDI inputs for more sources later.

Room presets

One touch sets the PA, mics, screens, cameras and outputs
Lecture. One technician, no console. Handheld mics run through Q-SYS at speech levels on the center and fills, the screen comes down, and the presenter's laptop shows on the projector and both side displays.
Theater. The DM7 takes over the mix, monitors and side fills come on, the screen comes down for scenic projection, and the cameras record the performance.
Concert. Full PA with subwoofers, performer monitoring, camera shots on the side displays, and the show recorded and streamed.
Stream & record. Cameras on M/E 1 go to the recorder and the stream with the house mix embedded; M/E 2 keeps the room's screens independent.
Zoom. Remote participants on the projector and side displays, the program camera sent to Zoom, and room mics and loudspeakers connected through Q-SYS.
System off. Mics, displays and projector off, screen up, amplifiers to standby. Startup reverses the sequence.
