Main conference room · Miami Airport

A conference room where every seat is heard and every screen works together.

Three ways to upgrade FTAI Aviation's main conference room, delivered by Mason Technologies with CDW. This page skips the parts list and shows how each option works: what people do in each kind of meeting, what each of the four screens shows, how the signals move, and what controls the room.

The room today

The challenge: a hard room and four screens that don't work together

Remote attendees can't hear the room

Painted block walls, a sealed concrete floor and few soft surfaces cause heavy reverberation. Clear voice pickup at every seat of the 15 ft table is the number one requirement, and it's how every option will be tested.

Four displays, no single system

Two 75″ touch displays on the north wall, plus 65″ displays on the east and south walls. All four stay. The goal is for every seat to see the people and the content.

A cluttered table and exposed cabling

Today there's a USB speaker puck, a keyboard and loose cables. Every option hides the equipment, keeps the table clean and runs new cable in white surface raceway without chipping the block walls.

Simple enough for guests to use unassisted

Few button presses and one obvious place to start. A Tap Scheduler outside the door shows when the room is free.

Existing conditions, drawn from site photos (north at the top). The floor conduit from the south wall to the table has room for only one or two more cables, which shapes every design.

Three options

Good, Better, Best

All three reuse the four displays, add wired and wireless sharing at the table, a room scheduler and acoustic panels, and hide the equipment. They differ in how the room hears, how it sees, and how you control it.

What this means day to day

  • Meetings are one touch. Joining, muting and sharing all happen on the table controller, just like other Teams rooms.
  • Screens follow Teams. The four displays copy the bar's two outputs, so the layout is whatever Teams shows. You can't send a different source to one screen.
  • The Dell needs a remote. To use the touch PC on a 75″, switch that display's input with its remote, then switch it back afterwards.
  • Power is per display. If a display is off or on the wrong input, its remote fixes it.
  • Easy to support. Teams Rooms on Android appliances are simple for local IT, and IP power conditioners let us reboot equipment remotely.
  • One place to start. Joining a meeting wakes the room, turns on the displays and sets the layout.
  • Toggle modes in one touch. Switch between Meeting, Present and Touch PC without touching a display.
  • Content follows the laptop. Plug in and the content screens switch automatically.
  • Room volume, not bar volume. Q-SYS balances the ceiling mic and ceiling speakers for the whole room.
  • A clean shutdown. Room Off powers the displays down so they aren't left on.

Hearing & seeing

How the room hears and sees you

In a reverberant room, a single bar on one wall can't reach the far end of a 15 ft table. Each option fixes that differently.

Microphone pickupCamera views

A second set of ears in the middle of the table

The Rally Bar covers the east end. Logitech Sight sits at the center of the table with seven beamforming microphones and a 7.5 ft pickup radius, which reaches both table ends. Its two 4K lenses give remote attendees close-up framing of people along the table, and Teams switches between the bar and Sight automatically.

Sight adds a device to the middle of the table. It's what makes the Good option hear and frame the whole table well. If the west-end seats test weak at commissioning, Logitech mic pods can be added.

Neat Center covers the table

The Neat Bar Pro on the east wall gives the room view. Neat Center at mid-table picks up voices around the table and gives remote attendees close-up views across it, which makes up for the bar sitting at one end.

There are no ceiling microphones in Better. Pickup depends on the bar and Center, and acoustic panels help them in this hard room.

Microphone in the ceiling, cameras on three walls

One Shure MXA925 ceiling array is rated for a 30 × 30 ft seating area, which is more than this room. Q-SYS handles echo cancellation and mixing, and four ceiling speakers spread remote voices evenly. Huddly Crew uses three cameras on the north, south and east walls, and its AI director cuts to the best angle of whoever is talking.

The table stays clear: no mic pods or tabletop cameras. Just the Tap and the connection cables.

Acoustic panels (all options)

Six 2×4 ft, 2″-thick fabric-wrapped panels reduce reflections from the block walls and concrete floor so the microphones get cleaner speech. They treat the room, not soundproof it. We don't use white-noise machines because they compete with the microphones.

Coverage shapes are illustrative. We'll confirm final microphone and camera positions with pickup tests at every seat during commissioning.

Signal flow

Where every signal goes

Table sources go into one auto-switcher.

Two HDMI cables, USB-C and AirMedia go into an auto-switcher under the table, which picks the newest source. One HDBaseT cable carries it through the floor conduit and up the east wall into the Rally Bar's HDMI input.

The Rally Bar is the Teams room.

It's the camera, speakers and microphones, connected to Microsoft Teams. Sight joins it over its own shielded cable from the table center, and the Tap IP controls the meeting over the network.

Two outputs feed four screens.

Output 1 (people) goes to a 1×4 splitter in a ceiling box, which feeds North-L, East and South. Output 2 (content) goes to North-R.

The Dell is a separate path.

The in-room Dell connects straight to a second input on each 75″, with a touch USB cable to each. You select it with the TV remote.

Table sources go into one auto-switcher.

Two HDMI cables, USB-C and AirMedia go into an auto-switcher under the table. One HDBaseT cable, the only new cable in the floor conduit, carries the signal to the Neat Bar Pro's HDMI input.

The Neat Bar Pro is the Teams room.

Camera, speakers and mics running Teams Rooms on Android. Neat Center and Neat Pad connect through a small network switch under the table that uses the existing table network connection.

Two outputs feed four screens.

Output 1 (people) goes through a 1×4 splitter to North-L, East and South. Output 2 (content) goes to North-R. The Bar Pro's third output isn't used because Teams doesn't support a third screen.

The Dell is a separate path.

Its two HDMI outputs and touch USB cables go straight to the 75″ displays. You select it with the TV remote.

Every table source is split two ways.

The auto-switcher sends one native 4K copy to the matrix for the room's screens and a 1080p copy into the Tap for remote attendees. Local content never goes through Teams compression.

The matrix routes any source to any screen.

It takes five inputs: two Teams outputs, the table, and the Dell's two outputs. Q-SYS sets every screen's source for each room mode.

Q-SYS runs the audio.

The Shure ceiling array connects over Dante to the Q-SYS Core, which removes echo and mixes the room. The Core connects to the Teams PC over USB and drives the amplifier and four ceiling speakers.

Huddly Crew handles the cameras.

Three cameras run on their own dedicated switch and connect to the Teams PC over USB. Crew picks and switches the shot automatically.

Control comes from the Tap.

Room Controls on the Tap tell Q-SYS which mode to run. Q-SYS sets the matrix routes and powers the displays on and off over IR.

Good to know

Details worth knowing before you choose

System notes

  • Teams Rooms on Android. Good and Better are Android appliances, and Neat only makes Android Teams Rooms devices. Please confirm FTAI IT doesn't require Windows.
  • Teams Rooms on Windows. Best runs on a Lenovo ThinkSmart Core with the Logitech Tap.
  • USB-C at the table is video only. It doesn't charge laptops or connect USB devices. Laptops need USB-C video output (DisplayPort Alt Mode), and so do the AirMedia pucks.
  • The Dell stays in the room. Its content reaches remote attendees only when it's shared from Teams on the Dell itself.
  • Touch works when the Dell's input is active and fills the screen.
  • Touch is wired to the Dell. Switch to Touch PC mode before touching, because touches on another source still reach the Dell.
  • Huddly cameras are black and the Tap is graphite. The ceiling microphone and speakers are white.
  • Neat Pad is black only.
