Conference

A Microsoft Teams meeting on the 220-inch wall. People in the room join, mute and move cameras on the Yealink controller they already know.

  1. Pick Conference on the Cyviz touch panel at the rack.
  2. Join the meeting on the Yealink controller, as today.
  3. Far-end video and shared content fill the wall; the side display shows whatever you pick for it.
  4. Share a PC, a laptop or the whole wall into Teams; content is held at a steady 1080p so remote sites don't flicker.

Training

Instructors plug into a wall plate or share wirelessly with an AirMedia puck. Remote trainees can join over Teams.

  1. Pick Training.
  2. Plug into the back-wall or side-wall HDMI plate, or press the AirMedia puck on your laptop.
  3. Pick that input on the touch panel. It takes over the wall; Teams can stay up in a corner window.
  4. Send the same slides to the side display for people seated along the side wall.

EOCPIN

Emergency operations: the three operations PCs, live TV and Teams on the wall at once, in a primary-source layout or a quad view.

  1. Pick EOC and enter the PIN. Only authorised staff can change the wall.
  2. Choose a layout: Primary + 3 puts one source large, or Quad gives four equal windows.
  3. Drop any source into any window: surveillance, WebEOC, GIS, IPTV news, Teams or a laptop.
  4. Put a different source on the side display, such as live news, without touching the wall.

Room Off

One button clears the room at the end of the day.

  1. Pick Room Off.
  2. Routing is cleared and room audio mutes.
  3. The LED wall powers down, where the wall's controller supports it.
  4. Next morning, pick any mode and the room comes back in one step.

Window contents are examples. Every layout and source assignment is set during commissioning with your team and can be changed on the touch panel.

System schematic

Follow every signal from source to screen.

Ten sources enter one AVPro Edge matrix. The Cyviz xpo5 turns seven of them into windows on the LED wall, the side display gets its own feed, and the Q-SYS Core mixes what you hear.

1.Pick a signal below.2.Follow the gold line through the rack.3.Read the steps underneath.

Teams meeting

Far-end video and shared content come out of the existing Yealink Teams Room and land on the wall. Far-end voices come out of the AVHub into the new Q-SYS Core.

  1. The Teams Room sends far-end video and shared content into matrix inputs 1 and 2.
  2. The matrix feeds them to xpo5 lanes; the xpo5 draws them as windows on the LED wall.
  3. Far-end voices leave the AVHub and enter the Q-SYS Core, which ducks program audio under speech.
  4. The Core feeds the existing Crown amplifier and the four Atlas pendants, zone by zone.

Operations PCs

Three owner micro PCs live on a shelf in the rack, one per matrix input, each with its own keyboard and mouse in the room.

  1. Each PC feeds its own matrix input (3, 4, 5) over a short HDMI patch.
  2. The matrix passes them to xpo5 lanes, so each can be its own window on the wall.
  3. Program audio is de-embedded at the matrix and mixed in the Q-SYS Core.
  4. Remote support is available for these PCs through the Cyviz desktop agent licence.

IPTV and live news

The IPTV box sits in the rack. It can be a window on the wall, on the side display, or both.

  1. The IPTV box feeds matrix input 6.
  2. Route it to an xpo5 lane for the wall, and/or to output 9 for the side display.
  3. Output 9 reaches the LG side display over an HDBaseT extender.
  4. Channel changes go over IP, or through the iTach IR adapter if the box needs IR.

Guest laptops

Two HDMI wall plates and an AirMedia receiver give visitors three ways in. There is no auto-switch: you pick the input on the touch panel.

  1. Plug into the back-wall or side-wall plate (inputs 8 and 9), or share with the AirMedia puck (input 7).
  2. Pick that input on the touch panel; it goes to an xpo5 lane on the wall.
  3. Send it to the side display as well for people seated along the side wall.
  4. Laptop audio plays through the room speakers via the Q-SYS Core.

Share the wall into Teams

Remote partners can see exactly what the room sees: one source, or the whole combined wall.

  1. The xpo5 sends its whole canvas back into matrix input 10.
  2. Matrix output 8 carries any source, or the canvas, to the Extron scaler.
  3. The scaler holds a steady 1080p so Teams doesn't re-sync each time a route changes.
  4. It enters the Teams Room's content input and goes out to remote sites.

One touch panel runs the room

The Cyviz controller talks to every device over a dedicated AV network that is kept apart from EBCI's production network.

  1. The 10.1″ touch panel sits on the rack top beside the Yealink controller.
  2. The Easy Controller X drives the matrix, the xpo5 and the Q-SYS Core.
  3. It also handles LED wall power where the wall supports it, the IPTV box and the WattBox outlets.
  4. Everything talks over the Netgear AV switch, isolated from the EBCI network.

Simplified. Port assignments follow the Rev 2 drawings and are confirmed during installation. On a phone, scroll the diagram sideways.
