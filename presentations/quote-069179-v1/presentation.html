How it works · System schematic

Two new Q-SYS endpoints connect the Teams Room to the existing room

One new encoder carries the Teams Room's video onto the Q-SYS network and gives Teams the room cameras, microphones and speakers over USB. One new decoder feeds any room source into Teams for content sharing. Everything else stays in service.

2 · Choose a view

The highlighted path shows which equipment does the work. Gold boxes are new from Mason; gray boxes are the City's existing equipment.

Join a Teams meeting

One-touch join from the room calendar

  1. Walk in. The table controller already lists today's meetings from the room's Microsoft 365 calendar.
  2. Tap Join.
  3. Teams appears on all four displays. The ceiling microphones and speakers carry the call, and the front camera is live.
  4. Volume and mute are on the Teams console. The front camera (CAM-09) is live by default.

The Teams Room's single HDMI output goes out through new encoder ENC-28 to the room's four display decoders.

Share a laptop

Plug in and it shares

  1. Plug the laptop into the table cubby (HDMI or USB-C).
  2. Q-SYS detects the laptop and routes it through new decoder DEC-29 into the Teams Room's content input.
  3. The laptop appears in the meeting and on the room displays.
  4. To share the room PC instead, tap Room PC under Room Controls.

Content routing depends on the inputs the Teams Room supports. BYOD laptops keep USB access to the room camera and microphones.

Switch cameras

Front camera by default; rear camera from the service UCI

  1. The front camera (CAM-09) is live whenever the room wakes. No action needed.
  2. To use the rear camera (CAM-10), a power user opens the online service UCI in the Q-SYS UCI Viewer or a web browser on a City PC or tablet.
  3. The service UCI shows live previews of both cameras, with PTZ and presets. Pick a camera and it goes to Teams through the ENC-28 USB bridge.

One camera is live in Teams at a time. The table controller keeps a simple source page, so camera control lives on the service UCI. Multi-camera composition and automatic camera switching are not included.

Join a Zoom or Webex invite

Microsoft Teams Rooms Direct Guest Join

  1. The meeting organizer invites the room's resource account.
  2. The meeting appears on the table controller like a Teams meeting.
  3. Tap Join. The room joins as a guest, using the same displays, microphones and camera.

Depends on Microsoft platform features and City IT policy. Hosting Zoom or Webex from the Teams Room is not included; Zoom on the room PC still works. Try it and see the setup →

Use the room as today

One simple source page, modeled on the City's current panel

  1. On the table controller, open Room Controls: six large tiles. Teams, Laptop 1, Laptop 2, Room PC, TV and Media Player.
  2. Tap TV or Media Player to put it on all four displays. Tap Back to Teams to return.
  3. TV channels, the full audio mixer and the Q-SYS softphone are on the online service UCI. The new IR-28 controls the Comcast tuner again. An incoming softphone call shows Answer / Decline on the controller.

Zoom on the room PC is unchanged. Program audio and camera feeds to the Community Room are assumed unchanged.

Option 1 · Teams Room

Join any Zoom or Webex meeting the room is invited to

Through Microsoft's Direct Guest Join, the Teams Room joins third-party meetings as a guest, using the room's displays, cameras, ceiling microphones and speakers.

  • One tap from the room calendar, the same as a Teams meeting
  • The room must be invited: add its email address to the invite
  • Joins as a guest; host controls and some sharing features differ from a native Zoom or Webex room
Best for: meetings hosted by outside agencies and vendors
Option 2 · Room PC

Host or run a full Zoom meeting from the room PC

The existing room PC (PC-06) keeps working as it does today, with the room camera and microphones over its existing USB connection. Select Room PC on the Room Controls page.

  • Full Zoom features, including hosting with the City's Zoom license
  • Shown on all four displays, like any room source
  • No change to how Zoom is scheduled today
Best for: Zoom meetings the City hosts

One-time setup for Zoom and Webex

Done once, before commissioning. Mason handles the room; City IT handles the Microsoft 365 side, per the City's policies.

City IT · Microsoft 365

  1. Let the room mailbox keep external invite details, so the Join link survives:
    Set-CalendarProcessing -Identity <room> -ProcessExternalMeetingMessages $true -DeleteComments $false -DeleteSubject $false
  2. If Microsoft Defender Safe Links rewrites URLs, exempt the room mailbox or the Zoom and Webex join links.
  3. Allow the room's network to reach Zoom and Webex.

Mason · Teams Room

  1. Turn on Zoom and Webex meetings (Direct Guest Join) in the Teams Room settings.
  2. Send test Zoom and Webex invites to the room and join each from the table controller.
  3. Confirm camera, ceiling mics, speakers and mute during the call, as part of the acceptance tests.

Scope of work

What Mason delivers

Mason furnishes, installs, configures, programs, tests and commissions the Teams Room and integrates it with the existing Q-SYS system. The project is limited to Chief's Conference Room 3111.

2.0Microsoft Teams Room
  • Install and secure the Teams Room compute in the space behind the front display, subject to field verification.
  • Install the Lenovo IP Controller at the table in place of the Q-SYS touch panel, reusing the existing table network connection where compatible.
  • Configure the compute for native Teams, enroll it with the City's Teams Rooms Pro license and room resource account, and show scheduled meetings on the controller.
  • Room Controls on the controller is one page of six source tiles: Teams, Laptop 1, Laptop 2, Room PC, TV and Media Player. Volume and mute stay on the Teams console.
  • Coordinate device registration, network, authentication and security policies with City IT.
  • Remove the existing Q-SYS table panel and mounting hardware and return them to the City.
3.0Q-SYS video distribution
  • Teams out: one NV-21-HU encoder takes the Teams Room's primary HDMI output and sends it to all four displays through the existing decoders. It also provides USB bridging to the room's conferencing peripherals.
  • Content in: one NV-21-HU decoder feeds the Teams Room's content input from table laptops, the room PC or other Q-SYS sources.
  • Single Teams video output; independent layouts on several displays are not included. Existing displays are not replaced or moved.
4.0Audio and camera integration
  • Route the Core X20r, two Shure ceiling arrays, amplifier and eight ceiling speakers to Teams over the USB bridge: two-way audio, mic mute and room volume.
  • Verify audio levels, echo cancellation and routing during commissioning.
  • Integrate the two existing PTZ cameras: the front camera is live by default; front or rear selection, previews, PTZ and presets on the online service UCI. One camera live at a time.
5.0Q-SYS control programming
  • Back up the existing Q-SYS design before any change.
  • Add the new endpoints, IR-28 and USB bridging to the shared design.
  • Room Controls (Teams console): a Teams-style page of six source tiles, with laptop hot-plug auto-share.
  • Service UCI (online only, no physical panel): per-display routing, live camera previews, PTZ and presets, TV tuner, full mixer, softphone and PIN-protected settings, opened from the Q-SYS UCI Viewer or a web browser on City devices.
  • Keep the Q-SYS softphone and verify that existing sources still work.
  • Changes are limited to Room 3111 and deployed in coordination with City IT, since the Core X20r serves other areas.
6.0Remote monitoring

Install, register and configure the Cyviz Teams Rooms monitoring agent; set up health and diagnostic functions; coordinate remote-management permissions with City IT; verify connectivity. Includes a five-year subscription under the manufacturer's licensing terms.

Remote monitoring is not unlimited technical support, guaranteed incident resolution or an on-site service level agreement.

7.0Installation and infrastructure
  • Mount the compute, new endpoints and auxiliary power supplies; connect HDMI, USB, Ethernet and other low-voltage interfaces.
  • Install the QIO-IR1x4 (IR-28) behind the front display with an IR emitter on the Comcast tuner.
  • Reuse existing cabling and pathways where suitable; dress, identify and label all new cabling.
  • Final placement is confirmed during planning based on space, ventilation, power and cable access. Moving equipment to the IDF rack, if needed, is coordinated with the City and may need a change order.
8.0Testing, commissioning and turnover

Verify registration and calendar sync, one-touch join, Teams on the displays, laptop and room PC sharing, two-way audio and mute sync, front and rear camera selection, Room Controls, the online service UCI, tuner IR control, existing sources, monitoring and supported guest join.

Mason provides an operational walkthrough for City staff and updated documentation. Acceptance is a successful demonstration with a City representative.
