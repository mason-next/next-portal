01 · System Overview

One platform. Six zones. Every announcement heard.

Mason will furnish, install, program and commission a new outdoor paging and background music system for Building 1, with Q-SYS handling processing, routing, amplification, control and zone management.

Six independent zones

Page one zone, a whole pole, any combination, or all six at once for a building-wide announcement.

Three ways to page

A touchscreen station in the security office, a campus phone call, or pre-programmed messages.

Background music

A network music player feeds any zone, and pages take priority over it.

Built for outdoors

IP54 loudspeakers with powder-coated aluminum enclosures, stainless hardware and sealed inputs.

Clear speech across the plaza

Each column covers about 160° horizontally. Four per pole, each aimed individually during commissioning.

Room to grow

Six of the amplifier's eight channels are used, leaving two for future Building 1 zones.

03 · How Paging Works

The right message, to the right zone, every time.

All three paging methods feed the same Q-SYS Core, which decides which zones hear each announcement and which source takes priority.

01

Security office paging station

An officer picks a zone, a pole or all-call on the touchscreen, then presses push-to-talk and speaks into the handheld microphone.

NeedsOne PoE network connection at the wall-mount location.
02

VoIP phone page

Authorized staff dial an extension from a campus phone and speak the announcement.

NeedsA SIP extension from MDC's phone system, registered to the Q-SYS softphone.
03

Pre-programmed messages

Recorded announcements stored on the Core, started from the touchscreen, a schedule or an external trigger.

NeedsFinal message list and recordings from MDC.

  • Individual, grouped and all-zone paging
  • Paging station user interface
  • Paging priority and source management
  • Background music routing
  • Recorded-message playback
  • SIP softphone configuration
  • Amplifier channels and loudspeaker processing

Verified at commissioning

  • All twelve loudspeakers operating
  • Independent operation of all six zones
  • Group and all-zone paging
  • Paging station operation
  • Background music routing
  • Recorded-message playback
  • Q-SYS amplifier and DSP operation
  • SIP paging (with MDC IT services in place)
  • Paging priority over background audio

MDC provides

  • Three structurally suitable outdoor poles
  • Approval of mounting locations and aiming
  • Pathways, conduit and pull boxes
  • AC power at equipment locations
  • Network ports, VLANs, firewall rules
  • SIP extension, credentials and licensing
  • Internet and music-service accounts
  • Message recordings, schedules, zone names
To confirm before final design

Poles

Diameter, material and height of the MDC-furnished poles, so the adapter brackets can be built to fit.

Zone split

Which two loudspeakers on each pole form each zone, for example plaza side and building side.

Cable routes

Distance and pathway from the headend to each pole. The proposal carries 2,000 ft of speaker cable.

Equipment location

Rack space, power and cooling for the amplifier, Core, music player and switch.

VoIP integration

A SIP extension and network access from MDC IT for phone paging.

Message list & network

The announcements MDC wants and their triggers, and whether the system runs on its own switch or joins MDC's network.
